Other galaxies we know

The observable universe holds hundreds of billions of galaxies. These are the ones that are brightest, closest or most studied — several are visible from a dark back garden with nothing more than binoculars.

Photographs are open-source imagery hosted on Wikimedia Commons: public-domain NASA/ESA Hubble releases and Creative Commons images from ESO and other observatories.
